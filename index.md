# Privacy Policy for HSA Expenses

_Last updated: October 9, 2026_

HSA Expenses ("the app") helps you keep track of medical expenses you may pay for or reimburse from a Health Savings Account. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: **your information stays on your device, or in your own iCloud account if you turn on iCloud Sync. We don't collect it, see it, sell it, or share it.**

## Who we are

The app is made by Sean Howard, an independent developer. If you have questions about this policy, open an issue at https://github.com/SMH84/hsa-expenses-privacy/issues.

## What information the app stores

When you use the app, you may enter:

- **Expense details:** amounts, dates, categories, notes, and whether an expense has been reimbursed.
- **Patient details:** names, relationships (for example "Self" or "Child") and, if you choose, dates of birth for you and your family members.
- **Receipts:** photos, scans and PDF files you attach to expenses.
- **Places:** the name and address of a provider or pharmacy you pick for an expense.
- **Mileage trips:** for each drive, where it started and ended (a place's name and address, the address of your current location if you chose it, or just "Home"), the miles, and any parking or tolls.
- **App preferences:** such as your color theme and date format.

Some of this is health-related information. We have no account system, no servers of our own, and no way to view your data.

## Where your information is kept

- **On your device.** By default, everything is stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone.
- **In your iCloud account, if you choose.** If you turn on iCloud Sync (during setup or in Settings), the app stores a copy in your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, so it can appear on your other devices. That copy belongs to your Apple Account and is protected by Apple. We can't see it. You can turn iCloud Sync off at any time, and while it's off you can delete the iCloud copy from Settings > iCloud Sync.
- **Your home address, if you set one for mileage.** It's saved on your device and in your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage), so it's available on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. It's never part of family sharing, and mileage trips record it only as "Home". Your mileage settings are kept the same way.

## Family sharing

If you create a family in the app and invite someone, the expenses, patients, categories and receipts in your family are shared with the people you invite, through Apple's iCloud sharing. Each person sees and can change the shared information according to the role you give them (Admin, Member or View Only). The people in the family can see each other's names as provided by Apple. You can change roles or remove someone at any time, and members can leave. We are not part of the family and can't see the shared information.

## Permissions the app asks for

The app only asks for these when you use the feature that needs them, and you can say no or change your mind at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

- **Location (while using the app):** used to show nearby providers first when you search for where an expense happened, and, when you choose Current Location for a mileage drive or your home address, to find where you are. That place's address is saved with the trip (or as your home address). The app doesn't otherwise save your location. The place you choose for an expense (its name and address) is saved with that expense.
- **Camera:** used only to scan receipts.
- **Photos:** used only to attach a receipt photo you pick.

## Apple services the app uses

- **Apple Maps:** when you search for a place, your search text and approximate area are sent to Apple to find matching places, as with any app that uses Apple Maps. When the app works out the miles for a drive, its start and end points (which can include your home address or current location) are sent to Apple Maps to get the driving distance.
- **iCloud:** only if you turn on iCloud Sync or family sharing, as described above. The app also downloads the current IRS mileage rates from the app's public iCloud database. That's a download only; nothing about you is sent.
- **On-device intelligence:** reading receipts and the Insights summary use Apple's on-device text recognition and Apple Intelligence. This runs on your iPhone and nothing is sent anywhere for it.

Apple's handling of data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## What we don't do

- We don't collect analytics or usage data.
- We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
- We don't track you across other apps or websites.
- We don't sell or share your information with anyone.

## Exporting and sharing files

The app can create CSV or PDF exports of your expenses. An export is only shared when you choose to share it (for example, by email or saving to Files). Once you share a file, it is handled by the app or person you sent it to.

## Backups

Your data may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, if you have backups turned on. Those backups are managed by Apple and your own settings, not by us.

## Deleting your data

You can delete individual expenses, receipts and patients inside the app. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. If you used iCloud Sync, delete the iCloud copy from Settings > iCloud Sync (with sync turned off) before deleting the app, or remove it later in your iPhone's Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage. Information you shared with a family stays available to the family until you remove it or stop sharing.

## Children

The app is not directed at children. A parent or guardian may enter information about their children as patients; that information is kept the same way as everything else, as described above.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, we'll update this policy and the "Last updated" date before that version is released.
